Should I update this order?

Check before you click Modify. It compares updating the order with cancelling it and placing a new one.

Formulas used. Sales tax = 7.5% × (1 − 0.11 × Accounting). NPC broker fee = 3% − 0.3% × Broker Relations − 0.03% × faction standing − 0.02% × corp standing, minimum 1%. Order update = (1 − (50% + 6% × Adv. Broker Relations)) × broker fee × new order value, plus the full broker fee on any increase in order value. Order slots = 5 + 4×Trade + 8×Retail + 16×Wholesale + 32×Tycoon. Sources: EVE University: Trade, EVE University: Tax. Check them against your in-game market window after any patch.
