Profit is per unit after Jita cost, hauling, the hub broker fee and sales tax. "Daily volume" is the average units traded per day over 30 days in the hub's whole region, so treat it as a rough guide. Sell price is the hub's current lowest sell, so you'd be listing at or just under it.
Hub orders come from ESI using your login. Jita prices come from Fuzzwork's market aggregates for Jita IV - Moon 4. Fees: sales tax = 7.5% × (1 − 0.11 × Accounting); NPC broker fee = 3% − 0.3% × Broker Relations − 0.03% × faction standing − 0.02% × corp standing, minimum 1%.
