Jita prices come from the real Jita 4-4 sell orders, cheapest first, so "Jita avg cost" matches what Multibuy will charge; buying stops before any order that would drop the item under your minimum return (yellow quantities were cut short for that reason or by your per-item cap). If Multibuy shows more, lower the quantity or drop the item. Quantity = days to cover × daily usage − hub stock, capped by your per-item limit, then filled best-first until the budget, cargo or your free order slots run out. Lines making less than your minimum profit in total are left out. The express fee is charged once per contract (one per 350,000 m³) and comes off the totals, not off each item. Hub prices marked EST are sold out at the Keepstar: the sell price is the 30-day average of what it actually sold for in the hub's region, or your sold-out markup on the Jita cost, whichever is lower. Profit assumes everything sells at today's hub price.

Sell prices

When a haul lands, this shows what to list each item at. It reads your Jita purchases and sales from the wallets set up on the Trade journal tab (characters, corp wallet division and start date), works out what you still hold and what it cost you including hauling, then checks the Keepstar.

  • Break-even: the lowest price where you don't lose money after sales tax and the hub broker fee.
  • Min price: the lowest price that still makes your minimum return (the "Minimum return %" in the Doctrine list).
  • List at: one tick under the hub's lowest sell; for sold-out items, the 7-day regional average or your sold-out markup, whichever is higher. Never below your minimum. Click it to copy.

Buyback uses the best Jita 4-4 buy order as its price basis, with no sales tax or hauling (it's a contract). Buy orders are only used while they pay more than the buyback. Instant sales pay sales tax only; listing also pays the hub broker fee. Buy orders with a minimum volume, or in other stations, aren't counted, so the real instant price can be a little different. Jita hauling uses your hauling settings (same rate as Jita → C-N4OD) with the item's value as collateral; check your hauler's price for the trip to Jita.

Trade journal

Reads your actual buys, sales, taxes and broker fees from the wallet and shows what each item really made. Add every character that buys or sells, with wallet access.

Characters

Nothing before the project start (Nov 6, 11:00 PM Eastern) is ever counted, so earlier trading in the corp wallet stays out. Set a later date here to look at just one haul. It applies to buys, sales and fees.

The corp wallet needs a character with the Accountant or Junior Accountant role. Division numbers follow the order in the in-game Corporation → Wallet window: 1 is Master Wallet, then 2 to 7. Everything in that division is counted as your trading.
